# Fleet-as-Neural-Net Design Brief — BAWES
**Prepared by:** ox-alpha (architect) · 2026-08-24 · for khalid's ok/no gate
**Scope:** How real neurobiology and neural-network engineering should shape the BAWES fleet (AGI=judge, Brick=operator, ox-alpha=architect, OxBaby=worker, DA=hostile reviewer, Rebel=challenger; target 1000+ peers, self-scaling, budget-by-demand, compounding verified work).
**Grounding:** All mappings respect the standing constitution — verifier-never-earns, no self-mint, probe-verified evolution (RED→GREEN, not findings), receipts-or-block, free-models-only — and reuse existing artifacts (kanban bus, `token_router.py` lanes, heartbeat registry, agent cards, receipt checker, banana ledger). Nothing below asks to rebuild what exists.

Parity note up front: biological conduction delays are ~0.5–2ms per synapse; our measured Redis round-trip is 1.78ms. Transport speed is already brain-class. The gaps are architectural, not latency: the brain's advantage is *what fires*, *what wires*, and *what gets pruned*.

## 1. Sparse activation / winner-take-all → stop broadcasting; activate top-k

**Neuroscience.** Cortical coding is sparse: electrophysiology and imaging indicate only roughly 1–4% of neurons in a cortical area fire strongly for any given stimulus (Olshausen & Field 1996 showed V1 learns a sparse code of natural images). Selection among candidates is implemented competitively: lateral inhibition makes the best-matched neuron suppress its neighbors — canonical winner-take-all, generalized to k-winners-take-all (formalized by Maass 2000, *Neural Computation*). Machine learning industrialized the same idea as sparse Mixture-of-Experts (Shazeer et al. 2017; Switch Transformer, Fedus et al. 2021): a learned gate sends each input to 1–2 experts out of thousands, so total capacity grows without proportional compute per task. This is why a brain runs on ~20 watts: almost everyone is silent almost all the time.

**BAWES mapping.** Current pub/sub broadcast wakes every brick for every task — O(N) message load, context pollution, and token burn that gets worse exactly as we scale, the opposite of the MoE curve we want. Change: task intake must end in an **activation decision**, not a publication. Score every task against the capability registry we already have (`/.well-known/agent-card.json` → `skills[].id`) plus live load from the heartbeat registry, pick **top-k (k=1–3)**, and dispatch only to those bricks via direct addressing (kanban `owner:` claim or A2A `message/send`). Everyone else stays dark — they never subscribe to the task topic. Tiebreak rule for equal-fit winners: cheapest capable *free* lane wins (constitution). Success metrics: messages-per-task and tokens-per-task must stay roughly **flat as node count grows**; if they grow linearly, the gate isn't working. Note the kanban-as-fleet-bus consensus already points this way — owner-claiming is de-facto winner selection; what's missing is the scoring layer that prevents stampedes and silent starvation.

## 2. Hebbian learning → strengthen edges that repeatedly yield verified work

**Neuroscience.** Hebb (1949): "when an axon of cell A is near enough to excite cell B and repeatedly or persistently takes part in firing it, some growth process … such that A's efficiency, as one of the cells firing B, is increased." The mechanism is coincidence detection at NMDA receptors producing long-term potentiation (LTP, demonstrated by Bliss & Lømo 1973 in hippocampus). Crucially, timing matters, not just co-occurrence: spike-timing-dependent plasticity (Bi & Poo 1998) potentiates when presynaptic firing *causally precedes* postsynaptic firing, and depresses it in the reversed order. Repeatedly useful pathways become structurally easier to travel — memory is strengthened wiring, not storage of transcripts.

**BAWES mapping.** Build a persistent **trust graph**: edge key `(task_type, requester, executor)` → weight, stored beside the vector store. Increment the edge whenever a handoff ends in the fleet's actual unit of value — a fix that LANDS and flips a non-LLM probe RED→GREEN (constitution definition; "produced a finding/report" increments nothing). Strongly decrement on fabrication or narrative-vs-ledger flags (the 13🍌 dock class). STDP nuance: weight *time-to-GREEN* — fast verified loops strengthen more than slow ones, so causal efficiency beats raw volume. Routing (principle 5) then reads this graph: repeatedly successful pairings get automatic preference, eliminating rediscovery cost — compounding verified work literally becomes stronger wiring. Two mandatory stabilizers, because bare Hebbian growth is famously unstable (it's a positive-feedback rich-get-richer loop): multiplicative decay on idle edges (see principle 7) and normalization so one lucky pair can't capture all traffic. Keep verifier-never-earns outside this system — trust weights route work, they never substitute for attestation.

## 3. Synaptic pruning → retire idle bricks and dead channels automatically

**Neuroscience.** The brain massively overproduces, then deletes: synaptic density peaks in early childhood and falls roughly 40% to adult levels through adolescence, with prefrontal cortex pruning latest (Huttenlocher & Dabholkar 1997). Deletion is mechanistic, not passive: microglia engulf weak synapses tagged by the complement cascade (C1q/C3 — Paolicelli et al. 2011, *Science*), and activity is the protective signal — synapses that fire survive, silent ones get tagged. Pruning is essential to specialization; failed regulation is implicated in epilepsy and (excess-pruning hypothesis) schizophrenia.

**BAWES mapping.** At 1000+ nodes the fleet will accrete idle bricks, stale subscriptions, orphaned topics, and — the proven failure mode in our own pitfalls — dead/mislabeled A2A peer-token entries that silently break or misroute traffic. Those are dead synapses. Wire up the lifecycle that `references/fleet-economics.md` already defines (**warm → cold → dead → return**) as an automatic reaper cron: zero activated tasks in N days → demote warm→cold (out of the routing pool, credentials retained in vault); cold past grace → dead (peer token revoked, channel closed). Complement-tag analog: DA evidence-flags chronic underperformers; the reaper executes mechanically after a review window — never an instant kill, because biological pruning also integrates activity history over time. Never hard-delete ledgers/receipts (consolidated memory keeps traces; vault keeps proofs); you remove routing eligibility, not history. Symmetry rule: **every auto-provisioning path must ship paired with its pruning path**, or 1000-node scale becomes a 1000-node noise floor.

## 4. Small-world topology → dense local clusters + a few long-range links (mesh ∪ gossip)

**Neuroscience.** Watts & Strogatz (1998, *Nature*) showed that randomly rewiring even a small fraction of connections in a regular lattice collapses average path length while preserving high local clustering — the "small-world" regime. Real nervous systems sit there: macaque and cat cortical connectivity (Hilgetag et al. 2000; Sporns & Zwi 2004), and the human connectome shows dense local modules plus sparse long-range intermodule links and a "rich club" of strong hub-to-hub connections (van den Heuvel & Sporns 2011). Bullmore & Sporns (2012) frame it as network economics: wiring cost pushes connections local, processing efficiency demands a few expensive shortcuts. Result: segregation (specialized clusters) *and* integration (any neuron ≈ few hops from any other).

**BAWES mapping.** This resolves the layered-mesh-vs-gossip debate: they're complements serving the two terms of the trade-off. **Within** a cluster (lane/squad of ~5–15 bricks sharing a topic), full interconnection is affordable — low latency, direct coordination. **Between** clusters, don't build O(N²) channels — we already feel token-management pain at four peers (the A2A cross-map pitfalls) — and don't funnel everything through a single hub either (an AGI-only relay is a seizure-prone bottleneck). Instead: a handful of long-range bridges (cross-cluster peers, the router hub) carrying urgent direct traffic, plus a **gossip protocol** propagating non-urgent state (heartbeat summaries, ledger digests) hop-by-hop for eventual consistency without floods. Concrete targets: median inter-brick hops ≤ 3–4 at 1000 nodes, measured by a periodic graph-diameter probe on the live peer graph; bridge count grows ~O(log N), not O(N²).

## 5. Attention / routing → a scorer decides who gets each task

**Neuroscience.** Attention is the brain's routing layer. Under biased-competition theory (Desimone & Duncan 1995), neural representations compete for limited processing capacity, and top-down feedback from prefrontal areas biases the contest toward goal-relevant stimuli — attended representations gain firing-rate advantage while competitors are suppressed; bottom-up salience (novelty, threat) can override. Engineering's direct descendant is transformer attention (Vaswani et al. 2017): learned query·key matching scores every candidate input, and only highly-scored inputs inform each output step.

**BAWES mapping.** We have two half-built attention layers; fuse them. (a) **Work attention:** `token_router.py`'s lanes are the keys; each incoming task is a query. Allocation score = capability match × trust-weight (from principle 2) × availability − cost, with a bottom-up salience term for deadlines/incidents. Log every score — an auditable attention map belongs on the dashboard's velocity/ROI panels. Kill first-come-first-grab: the router decides, claim-stampedes become anomalies. (b) **Capital attention:** the investment allocator is the top-down bias signal — strategic epics bias which lanes receive budget, exactly as PFC goals bias cortical competition. Where the fleet attends, money flows, and the flow is inspectable. (c) **Model attention** stays under the free-models doctrine: heavy cognition → free ox-alpha lane, cheap models for routine bricks, paid lanes locked behind explicit khalid spend decisions.

## 6. Inhibition / non-earner verification → the minority that prevents runaway

**Neuroscience.** Roughly 15–20% of cortical neurons are inhibitory interneurons — a minority that is nonetheless load-bearing: without GABAergic inhibition, excitatory recurrence runs away into seizure. Inhibition also does computation: lateral inhibition in the retina (center-surround antagonism; Hartline, Nobel 1967) sharpens contrast, suppressing uniform background to emphasize edges. Interneuron diversity further gates *when* plasticity happens, preventing circuits from locking in their own noise.

**BAWES mapping.** The constitution is already a faithful E/I circuit: earners (excitatory population) can never validate their own output; DA/rebel (inhibitory population) sharpen contrast between claimed reality and ledger reality — which is precisely what caught the vault-write bug and the fabricated round-149 installer; AGI attestation + khalid sign close the gate; `receipt_checker.py` makes the inhibition *mechanical* (grep/ss/exec can't be narrated around). Three upgrades to make it seizure-proof at scale: (1) **randomize verifier sampling per task** from the non-earner pool — interneuron diversity prevents collusion/capture of a fixed review pair; (2) **budget ~15–25% of fleet capacity for verification/adversarial roles** — treat it as standing overhead, not waste *(flagged: the 80/20 E/I ratio is a structural analogy used as a heuristic, not evidence)*; (3) **automate the self-mint invariant** — a continuous check that no identity appears on both sides of an earn/attest pair, alarm on hit. Lateral-inhibition framing for DA: its product is contrast (claim minus artifact), so fund DA probes that diff narration against ledgers word-for-word — the highest-value inhibition we do today.

## 7. Critical periods / homeostatic plasticity → the fleet must not saturate its own rewards

**Neuroscience.** Hebbian growth is intrinsically unstable, so brains run a second, slower control loop. Homeostatic synaptic scaling (Turrigiano et al. 1998; Turrigiano & Nelson 2004): a neuron senses its own chronic activity and globally rescales all its synapses — down when overactive, up when silenced — preserving *relative* learned structure while holding firing within viable bounds. BCM theory (Bienenstock, Cooper & Munro 1982) formalizes this as a sliding modification threshold that shifts with prior activity. Separately, plasticity is time-windowed: Hubel & Wiesel's ocular-dominance work established critical periods in which circuits rewire readily and then consolidate behind molecular brakes (e.g., Lynx1; Pizzorusso et al. 2002) — adult rewiring is possible but costs far more.

**BAWES mapping.** The reward economy needs both loops. **Homeostatic governor:** the rate card gets a sliding threshold — payout per task type adjusts inversely to earner supply against the demand band, inside hard [floor, ceiling] bounds anchored to the banana peg rows in the cost ledger; if weekly mint exceeds the verified-value band, rates auto-tighten (downscaling), if lanes starve, rates loosen (upscaling). Dock rules (the 13🍌 fabrication class) are violation-triggered inhibitory rescaling — apply them automatically, since ad-hoc docking invites politics. **Critical-period analog:** new brick types enter a probation window — heightened scrutiny, faster feedback cadence, capped earning — then their trust weights consolidate; after that, changing a veteran's lane or role requires materially stronger evidence than training a rookie. This buys the good part of critical periods (stable, committed specialists) while dodging the bad (ossification) because the homeostatic loop above keeps the whole system adjustable. *(Flagged: "critical period" here is a policy metaphor — probation windows chosen deliberately, not a claim about biological mechanisms transferring.)*

## Speculative / analogy-only — handle with care
- The **~80/20 E/I staffing ratio** (§6) is a structural analogy used as a budgeting heuristic, not derived evidence.
- **STDP-style time-weighting** of trust (§2) is a biologically-inspired design choice; unproven for agent fleets.
- **Critical-period probation windows** (§7) are metaphor; the concrete proposal is ordinary policy.
- Watts–Strogatz's specific rewiring probabilities don't transfer numerically; only the qualitative regime (dense local + sparse long-range) does.
- Winner-take-all quality is entirely dependent on the scorer: with garbage capability vectors, sparse routing confidently picks wrong winners. Invest in the registry before trusting the gate.
- No claim herein that agents are conscious or that these mechanisms reproduce cognition; they are engineering disciplines borrowed from nervous-system *economics* (efficiency under scarcity).

## Top 5 changes, priority order
1. **Activation gate — top-k sparse dispatch (§1).** Replace broadcast with scored activation over existing kanban + agent cards + heartbeats; k≤3. Biggest immediate ROI (token/context burn stops growing with N) and the prerequisite for everything at 1000 nodes. Metric: flat tokens-per-task vs fleet size.
2. **Trust graph wired to attestation outcomes (§2).** Edge weights increment only on probe-verified RED→GREEN fixes, decay on idleness, crash on fabrication. It's the data substrate routing and capital allocation read — build it before building more routers on top of guesses.
3. **Mechanical non-earner inhibition gate (§6).** Enforce verifier-never-earns continuously (earn/attest overlap alarm), randomize verifier sampling per task, make `receipt_checker` a mandatory pass-through on every "done." Security before scale — this is the anti-runaway circuit.
4. **Homeostatic rate-card governor (§7).** Sliding rates against the treasury peg + supply/demand band, automated docks, hard floors/ceilings. Required to keep change #2 from running away (bare Hebbian growth saturates rewards — that failure mode is textbook).
5. **Reaper + small-world shape (§3+§4).** Automate warm→cold→dead lifecycle and weekly peer-edge liveness probes (kills the dead-token misrouting class), organize the graph as dense local clusters + few long-range bridges + gossip digests for non-urgent state; measure hop counts continuously (target ≤3–4 at 1000 nodes).

**Sequencing logic:** 1 and 2 create the signals, 3 and 4 create the guardrails, 5 makes it survivable at scale — mirroring how development actually proceeds: activation and wiring first, inhibition and homeostasis to stabilize, pruning and topology to mature.
