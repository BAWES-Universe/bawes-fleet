## 6. Risk flags (primary-source, strongest section — CBK PDFs)
- **Capital walls:** Kuwait EPIP needs paid-up capital ≥ KD 1M + KD 100K bank guarantee; final settlement must route through local banks.
- **Fee constraint:** EPIPs/agents may NOT charge end-customers any fee → merchant-side/B2B monetization only.
- **SAMA:** crowded (32 licensed cos) but PTSP category open to foreign orchestrators (Yuno precedent).
- **What kills fintechs here:** capital/guarantee walls, interchange-floor unit economics vs 20bps ambitions, bank settlement dependence, national switches (KNET/mada), 12–24mo licensing, funded entrants (Yuno, PayTabs+OGS, MoneyHash×Tap).
