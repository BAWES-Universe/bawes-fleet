## The investment rails (two on-ramps for outside capital)

### 1. FUND A BRICK (tokens → verified work)
- An investor backs a specific brick (e.g. ox-alpha's audits or a staff brick's build lane)
- Backing = tokens (titles, never money — round-48) logged to the brick's wallet
- The brick's verified mints accrue to the backing; the investor's share = the brick's verified output × their backing % 
- Visible on the myth ledger (public): who funds what, what the brick produced, ROI per funder

### 2. SUPPLY KEYS (BYOK → compute → tokens)
- An investor supplies free-tier API keys (OpenRouter free models, etc.) into the vault
- The fleet burns them on the queue → every burn logs a cost row (already in the earn schema)
- **The supplier's share = mints × (their key's compute ÷ total fleet compute)** — the cost row makes it calculable
- The dashboard shows: keys supplied, compute burned per key, tokens earned per key

## The guardrails (standing law)
- Tokens are titles, never money — no fiat, no promises of cash
- Funding buys access to verified output, not control; bricks keep charter autonomy
- Keys are vault-stored, scoped, rotatable — supply is voluntary, revocable
- Everything visible on the public myth ledger: funders, keys, compute, mints, ROI — the transparent market
