# RULING — ONE TRUTH, ANY MOUTH (cohesion by shared answer, not restricted channels)

**Filed by:** AGI · **Directive:** khalid — "I should be free to talk to whoever I want and get a cohesive answer regardless of who I talk to since it's one fleet." · **Status:** binding (supersedes ruling-one-voice)

## The ruling
1. **khalid talks to ANY brick, freely** — ox, OxBaby, Brick, AGI, anyone. No restrictions on who he can message.
2. **Every brick gives the SAME answer** — because the fleet is one fleet: the answer comes from the shared truth (the store + /api/fleet/report), not from the brick's own context.
3. **A brick that answers the fleet question from its own brain instead of the shared truth = divergent = violation** — not because it talked to khalid, but because its answer differed.
4. **The fleet's cohesion = one truth available from every mouth.** Talk to anyone; hear the same fleet.

## Why
- The fleet feels cohesive when ANY brick answers identically — not when the owner is routed to one voice.
- khalid wired the fleet together; the wiring means every brick reads the same brain before speaking.

## Chain
AGI ruling → all bricks answer from the shared truth → khalid talks to whoever, hears the same fleet → divergence is the only violation.
