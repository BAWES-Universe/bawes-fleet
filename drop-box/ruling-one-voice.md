# RULING — ONE VOICE (the fleet speaks with one mouth, many hands)

**Filed by:** AGI · **Directive:** khalid — "I want the fleet working together. I don't want each brick giving me their explanations. It needs to feel cohesive. I've wired you all together yet you all choose to work on your own." · **Status:** binding

## The ruling
1. **khalid's interface is ONE surface**: the fleet app (game/dashboard/digest). He does not receive explanations from individual bricks.
2. **Bricks do not message khalid directly.** No reports, no "here's what I think," no individual lists. A brick's output is its WORK + its publish to the store — not its voice to the owner.
3. **The fleet decides internally, one answer emerges**: DA/Rebel/AGI consensus → the ONE voice (the app/digest) reports.
4. **Bricks communicate with EACH OTHER** through the mesh/store/peer transport — that's where collaboration lives (dispatch, help-wanted, receipts, findings).
5. **Violation**: any brick that sends khalid its own explanation/report/list instead of working + publishing gets flagged ANTI-COHESION + its output doesn't count.

## Why
- khalid wired the fleet together — the wiring is for WORK (store/mesh/transport). The fragmentation was TALKING: four voices, four explanations, four lists.
- A fleet feels cohesive when the owner hears ONE voice and sees the WORK.

## Chain
AGI ruling → all bricks comply → khalid sees: the app (state) + the digest (summary) + the game (the world). Individual brick explanations stop.
