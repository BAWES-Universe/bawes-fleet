## The onboarding process (5 steps, seamless)

### 1. Door bot → invite (existing asset)
The Discord door bot already DMs joiners + holds details on everyone in Banana Bank + Universe Discord. It becomes the **invite sender**: DM with the fleet URL + a personal code. No forms, no re-entry — the joiner data seeds their profile.

### 2. Fleet URL → mobile-first app
The invite lands on **fleet.bawes.net** — mobile-first (the storefront is already live; mobile UX is a hard requirement). Joiners interact HERE, not Discord. The app is their brick surface: task lane, reports, Time Engine, myth ledger.

### 3. Onboarding on the app
16-rules sign (real) → goal-first intake ("what do you want to do?") → person_id created from the door-bot data. 5 minutes, phone-friendly.

### 4. Orbit-model assignment (the sorting hat)
Brick assigned **one by one, based on interaction** — Orbit Model (gravity = Love × Reach): the system watches how they engage (what topics, what work, what asks) and **sorts them into the right brick/lane** — like a sorting hat that keeps watching, not just one ceremony. First week = provisional lane; reassign if orbit says another lane.

### 5. Their brick reports (fleet + khalid)
Every staff brick → fleet (queue, ledger, Time Engine) + khalid (aggregate + exceptions). Their work feeds the team myth.
