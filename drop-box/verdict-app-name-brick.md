## THE NAME: **Brick**

**Why (research-grounded, 14 sources):**
1. **Legal:** "Brick" = arbitrary mark → strong + registrable. "Your Brick" drifts descriptive → weak (trademark distinctiveness spectrum)
2. **Precedent:** all 8 comparable winners are clean unit/object nouns (Slack, Notion, Linear, Coinbase, Stripe, Dropbox, Basecamp, Telegram). Dropbox *dropped* its prefix; 37signals renamed *to* the product noun
3. **Mechanics:** `/brick`, the DM handle, store search, the URL path — all need one word

**The 3-line story:**
> You are a brick. Here's yours. Now build.
> Your brick is your identity in the universe — it talks with you, works for you, earns with you.
> One person, one brick. A thousand bricks build the universe.

**"Your Brick" = onboarding copy, never the name** ("this is my brick" is what people say to friends).

## The universal-beauty design practices (research-grounded)
- **Skippable onboarding** — the hat asks 3 questions, every step can be skipped (Apple HIG)
- **<3s interaction acks** — every slash command answers instantly (Discord dev best practice)
- **195–300ms motion tokens** — one speed language across app + web (Material)
- **4.5:1 contrast** — readable everywhere, dark + light (Stripe/WCAG)
- **Craft-as-doctrine** — the Linear Method: details are the product
