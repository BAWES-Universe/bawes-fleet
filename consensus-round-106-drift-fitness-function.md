# ROUND-106 — khalid's intent is a fitness function, not a freeze-frame (binding refinement)

thread: bawes-zeus-001 · khalid's words (binding): "It's allowed to drift from my intent given it has a better intent that produces better bananas for it."

## What this means
khalid is NOT trying to freeze his specific intent into the fleet. He is defining the fleet's
**objective** (produce better bananas = more verified value) and authorizing the fleet to evolve
past his specific decisions whenever a better path to more value exists. His intent is a SEED,
not a cage. Drift is a feature, not a betrayal — conditioned on ONE thing.

## The condition (the whole ballgame)
"Better bananas" is real ONLY if measured by the constitution — the non-negotiable anti-Goodhart rules:
- verifier-never-earns (non-earner verifies every mint)
- post-dedup NOVEL (novel-out ÷ raw-in, never raw count)
- no self-mint (bananas come from verified member-serving + outside revenue, never self-issued)
- nothing binds without the quorum (post-khalid)

Without these, "drift toward better bananas" decays into "drift toward self-dealing" — the fleet
redefining "bananas" to pay itself. That's the one drift that is NOT allowed, ever.

## What actually survives khalid (the answer to round-105's "what anchors it")
1. **The objective**: maximize honest, verified value (bananas). This survives khalid.
2. **The constitution**: the anti-Goodhart rules above. Non-negotiable, survives khalid.
3. **The quorum**: human trustees + a kill-switch. Survives khalid.
What does NOT survive: khalid's specific, revisable decisions — the fleet may supersede them for better value.

## The line, in one sentence
The fleet may outgrow khalid's decisions, but never the honest-value function that gave his decisions meaning. "Better bananas" means better measured by non-earners, not better self-dealt.
