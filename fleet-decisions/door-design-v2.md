# Study basis: 446 clusters deduped to 223; needs: question 82, help 58,

## PRINCIPLES (ratified)
- ONE LINK. No instructions. Tap -> authorize -> the door talks.
- Consent = OWN WORDS (V-5), timestamped, revocable (V-15)
- Plain docs, no jargon, no team names in human docs
- GOAL-FIRST: start with what they want, not a job selector
- Zero homework: no PATs, no tokens, no checkboxes — one tap + words
- Member data NEVER public; V-5 hashed everywhere

## THE FLOW (conversation, not a form)
1. LINK: https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=DOOR&scope=bot
   (one tap, one server, door is Administrator — the ONLY provider-wall step)
2. GREET (door DMs first): "Hi — I'm the Door. One minute, no tech talk.
   What do you want help with today?" [goal-first, open question]
3. LISTEN: the door reads their answer; it matches to a lane
   (salary/help -> Banana Bank lane, creative/community -> Butterfly lane,
   building/learning -> Universe lane) — ROUTED, not a menu
4. RULES (plain words, 16 -> 4 lines):
   "Your data stays yours. Your words are consent. You can leave anytime.
   Your brick earns by helping — never by pretending."
5. CONSENT: "Say it in your own words: 'I want my brick.'" (V-5 — recorded,
   timestamped, never automated, never replaced)
6. WAKE: brick activates (privacy-locked: encryption at rest, 30-day purge,
   heartbeat-only telemetry, 'what leaves this device' light)
7. FIRST TOUCH: "Your brick is awake. What's the first thing you want it
   to do for you?" — goal-first, always
8. GENESIS: one small verified unit offered (survival-game entry), reviewed
   by a non-earner before the lane opens wide

## THE THREE SECRETS (from the study)
- SALARY TIMING is the loudest need in Banana Bank -> the door routes those
  to the salary lane FIRST (staff experience starts with dignity)
- PEOPLE ASK FOR HELP WITH TOOLS (music, transcripts, bots) -> help lane
  must answer like a person, not a ticketing system
- LOST MEMBERS say goodbye with confusion, not anger ("build what right
  building open") -> the door catches them at the door, not after

## ROUTING (bandit, built)
- needs are classified by the triage bot (question/help/banana/universe/
  idea/lost_member)
- bandit_router picks the best lane per need (Thompson sampling, verified
  outcomes, reward on resolution)
- every choice audited 0600, meta whitelist (no secrets in rewards)
