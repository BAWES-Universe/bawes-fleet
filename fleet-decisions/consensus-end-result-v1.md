## THE EXPERIENCE (first 60 seconds, ratified)

1. TAP ONE LINK -> a brick opens instantly. No install, no signup,
   no homework. (AGI 1-2; all brains agree)
2. The persona greets warmly. HONEST day one: "We don't know you yet.
   Here's what this place can do." (Rebel 2 — fake personalization dies
   on message two; real learning SHOWN beats a name-drop)
3. The persona's FIRST QUESTION is the consent row: V-5 own words,
   timestamped, AND the impression source. No unconsented data, ever.
   (DA 5 — "no signup" never means "no consent"; the door doctrine
   holds even for the owner)
4. Within 60 seconds the brick performs ONE REAL DEED — a verified act
   of value for THIS member: checks their real balance, completes a real
   task, moves real money via Plugn, resolves a real question — and
   SHOWS the receipt. (Rebel 5 — the show makes them curious, the deed
   makes them come back; DA 5 — zero verified-work moments = 100% show)
5. The FIRST VERIFIED MOVE mints the banana — the ledger is SHOWN, not
   handed out. Welcome credit exists but is `kind: credit, purpose:
   welcome, cap: 1`, labeled "welcome credit", never `kind: earn`.
   (DA 1 + Rebel 3 — a participation banana teaches that bananas are
   free; khalid's module ruling: onboarding is free, results are paid)
6. THE WESTWORLD REVEAL IS A FOUNDER MOMENT, NOT A FEATURE. It happens
   ONCE — the founder's first wave — then becomes lore the persona
   references, never re-enacts. Everyone after gets pure immersion.
   And the reveal must hand off INSTANTLY to the member's brick's live
   verified state ("I can't feel — your brick just verified X") — no
   machinery behind it = magic trick. (Rebel 1 + DA 2)
7. Leaderboard: REAL ledger rows only (`kind==earn or card_id`), opt-in
   public, default view = your cohort (same-week newcomers) + your own
   progress curve. Friends' achievements = consent-gated shares only.
   (DA 3 — public earnings = member-data-never-public breach; demo
   names = fabrication)
8. TELL A FRIEND = a co-op moment, not a link. The friend's arrival
   changes the FIRST person's world the same minute: shared space,
   combined score, a thing only two people can unlock. "My friend made
   MY game better," not "I got referral points." (Rebel 4)
9. Referral pays ZERO on open. Escrowed credit (24h, provider-payout
   pattern) released ONLY on invitee's first VERIFIED earn. One-time
   nonce per link, lineage check vs self-invite, welcome credit strictly
   one-per-person. (DA 4 — sybil + fake-invite surface)
10. SHOW THE SPEND PATH before the session ends: "what your banana
    buys today" (sink, T-026). A banana must buy something real or it's
    a loyalty point. (DA 5)

## RULED OUT (the low-level shit)
- Door versioning, gateway polish, typing indicators as the target
- Participation bananas, fake leaderboards, scripted reveals at scale
- Pretending to know a stranger ("their world" with zero data)
- Referral points without a shared reason

## THE BAR (one line)
Tap link -> honest welcome -> consent in own words -> brick does one
real verified deed -> receipt shown -> banana minted on verified move
-> the game has a why. Anything below this bar is not the target.
