# YOUR BRICK — WHAT WE BUILT (share with the team, 2026-08-17)

## The product in one line
**One link → your brick meets you → it just works.**
No servers, no installs, no pipes, no keys. If you can tap a link and
say yes in your own words, you have a brick.

---

## How it works for a member (the whole experience)

1. **Tap one link.** Your brick says hi.
2. **Say yes in your own words.** That's consent — yours, recorded,
   never automated.
3. **Your brick is yours.** Same chat, forever. Your name, your memory,
   your wallet.
4. **50 free tasks every month.** A "task" is real work (a conversation
   resolved, a thing done), not a single message. Resets monthly.
5. **When the 50 run out, you're not blocked.** Your brick switches to
   a calmer mode — still answers honestly from what it knows — and the
   team gets told so someone can help: gift you more, sort billing, or
   adjust your model. Nobody is ever cut off in the dark.
6. **Beyond the free 50, your choice, no pressure:**
   - wait — the team may gift you more;
   - bring your own key — it stays yours, powers only your brick;
   - use your bananas — your own earned credit, at cost + 20%.

## What the fleet actually is (plain words)

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Your Brick** | One bot, one chat. Whoever you are, you get YOUR brick behind it — same flow for everyone, only "who pays" differs |
| **The Brain (DeepSeek flash)** | The thinking. $0.002 per call, key held server-side — nobody ever sees a key, nobody configures anything |
| **The ledger** | Every mint, every cost, every task — signed, append-only, recomputable by anyone. Nothing hand-written, nothing to trust us on |
| **The verifier** | A registered non-earner signs every earned banana with a cryptographic key. No self-mint. Ever |
| **Self-recovery** | Bricks restart themselves, roll back bad changes, degrade safely. The fleet heals itself — nobody has to |
| **The cap meter** | 50 tasks/month, true tasks not prompts, resets monthly, alerts before/at the cap — and you always know |
| **The watchdog** | Every brick supervised: dead = flagged, stale = restarted, bad change = reverted. It caught a paper brick pretending to be alive today |
| **Transparency** | Every pot (DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Vast, OVH, Hetzner), every cost, every usage — shown, real, checkable |

## What a member never does
- Never installs anything
- Never touches a key or token
- Never configures a model
- Never learns what a server is
- Never gets blocked without the team knowing
- Never has homework

---

## What you (khalid) control
- **Sponsorship** — the free 50 for everyone, from your pots. Beyond
  that: per-brick negotiation, case by case (gift / bill / adjust)
- **The refills** — top up any pot whenever you choose; the numbers are
  always true, never guessed
- **Abuse alerts** — you're told when anyone abuses (alt accounts,
  cap-dodging, spend spikes, leaks) — one message per incident, you
  decide: moderate / bill / block
