## The simple rules (ratified by DA + Rebel + AGI, awaiting khalid's sign)

1. **Everyone gets a brick, free.** Your first 50 chats every month are free —
   every month, a fresh 50. No payment, no card, no forms.
2. **When the 50 run out, your brick doesn't stop working.** It switches to
   a calmer mode — it still answers from what it knows, honestly — and the
   team gets told, so someone can help you: gift you more, or sort it out.
   Nobody is ever blocked in the dark. Ever.
3. **Beyond the free 50, you have choices — all optional, no pressure:**
   - wait — the team may gift you more;
   - bring your own key — it stays yours, powers only your brick;
   - or use your bananas — your own earned credit.
4. **Your brick thinks with our brain (DeepSeek flash).** Nothing to
   configure. If you ever want more, we'll talk — later, case by case.
5. **Everything is transparent.** Every pot, every cost, every usage —
   shown, real, checkable by anyone.
