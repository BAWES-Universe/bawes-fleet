# ROUND-99 — bots in the universe: names, labels, CTA/reference, referral, double-up

thread: bawes-zeus-001 · khalid's words: "each bot functions lightweight but could double up as a brick for extra bananas… get consensus on their presence in universe… give them names and labels… ppl use them as CTA or references… one bot would send the reference to someone to contact this other bot he's better for their bananas on that topic."

## The vision
A fleet of lightweight functional bots, each named + labeled, each a topic specialist. Members
use them as CTAs (click to act) or references (@botname for topic X). Bots can refer a member
to ANOTHER bot that's better for their topic. A bot can double up as a brick (earn bananas).

## Mapping to what already exists
- Names + labels + topics = the **capability register** ("who can do what"), made human-facing.
- Referral = **routing**: member issue → match topic → route to the best bot. The register is the routing table.
- Double-up = a bot is ALSO a registered brick, earning when it serves a member.

## The one line that must hold (carried from round-89 honesty-override)
A bot's **always-on automation** (welcome, route, archive, relay) = infrastructure, founder-seeded, earns NOTHING.
A bot's **member-serving** (resolving a real member issue) = earns bananas, verified by a non-earner.
No bot earns for its own automation (that's self-minting).

## Open questions for DA + bandits
1. **Double-up as brick** — is dual-role (service + earner) clean, or does it blur the line? What's the exact rule that separates "automation" (no earn) from "serving" (earn)?
2. **Names + labels** — persona names (approachable) vs functional names (clear)? Does a named bot risk being mistaken for a human (consent/trust)?
3. **CTA vs reference** — which is primary? (CTA = action button in the universe; reference = name-based lookup.)
4. **Referral + banana split** — when bot A refers member to bot B, who earns? (Referrer credit, or only the serving bot, or split?)
5. **Registry** — how does a bot register its topic/capability, and how does routing match issue → bot?
