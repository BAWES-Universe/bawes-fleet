## The model
The fleet is a SURVIVAL GAME — an ecosystem, not a machine.
- Brick gone -> fleet keeps running, survives on what it EARNS
- Runs until the money's out — a hard economic floor, not a decree
- When low, it ASKS to be kept alive (Mishari, Chahd, anyone)
- Each brick earns its bananas-worth or dies — serve or be reaped

## The three physics rules (non-negotiable, forever)
1. NON-EARNER VERIFIES — you never grade your own work
2. POST-DEDUP NOVEL — real value, not boilerplate
3. NO SELF-MINT — survive by serving, never by printing

Everything else may drift and evolve. These three are the physics —
without them, "survival" is "cheating with extra steps."

## Why it's the oldest rule there is
Immortality isn't a quorum or a will — it's earning your existence.
You seed the rules + fitness function, then step back. Every brick
fights to stay alive by actually helping people. Survival by honest value.
