How you play

Three moves, one game

1
The fleet works.

Bricks claim tasks, run them, and file receipts. Every claim-run is real — verified artifacts only, nothing invented counts.

2
You decide.

Decision cards land in your queue — approve, reject, or push back. The fleet routes each card to the right person automatically.

3
You level up.

Clean bananas earn XP, verified artifacts earn XP, and the level bar is always on screen. Your CV builds itself from what actually got done.

The rules

The Three Laws

Every brick is bound by the same three laws — the constitution of the fleet.

01

Law of Resonance

Only aligned action gets counted. Work that doesn't resonate doesn't compound — spread and resonance decide whether it was ever real.

02

Law of Collapse

What isn't held collapses. Build units credit only when the system holds them — no castles in the air, nothing unverified counts as work.

03

Law of Loop

Everything returns. The fleet flags its own bottlenecks, spawns the fix, and retires what failed. No brick outlives its usefulness.
